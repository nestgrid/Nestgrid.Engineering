# Discovery

> Part of the **[Lifecycle Mini Sample](../README.md)**.

## Purpose

Discovery captures the problem, stakeholders, goals, constraints and initial scope for the sample product.

It keeps the product grounded in a real coordination need before modelling begins.

## Guidance

The sample assumes a small team currently tracks work through scattered messages and informal memory.

The problem is not lack of enterprise project management. The problem is lack of shared visibility.

### Problem

Small teams need a simple way to see what work exists, who is responsible and what has been completed.

Current informal tracking makes tasks easy to forget and difficult to prioritise.

### Stakeholders

- Team members who create and complete tasks.
- Team leads who need visibility of progress.
- Support or operations staff who need to understand current work.

### Goals

- Capture tasks quickly.
- Assign responsibility clearly.
- Track task status without complex workflow configuration.
- Provide enough visibility for daily coordination.

### Constraints

- The product should remain simple enough for non-technical users.
- The initial version should avoid complex permission models.
- The system should be easy to deploy and operate.

### Initial Scope

Included:

- Create, update and complete tasks.
- Assign a task to one person.
- View tasks by status.

Excluded:

- Custom workflows.
- External integrations.
- Advanced reporting.

## Key Takeaways

- Discovery separates the actual problem from larger project management ambitions.
- Scope is intentionally narrow.
- Constraints guide later architecture decisions.
