# Decisions

> Part of the **[Lifecycle Mini Sample](../README.md)**.

## Purpose

The decisions stage records significant choices made while applying the lifecycle to the sample.

Decision records keep the reasoning visible for future readers.

## Guidance

The sample records only decisions that materially affect direction.

Small implementation choices are intentionally omitted to keep the sample focused.

### Recorded Decisions

| ID | Title | Type | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ADR-001](decisions/ADR-001-use-modular-monolith.md) | Use Modular Monolith | Architecture | Accepted |

### Decision Rationale

The first recorded decision explains why the sample starts with a modular monolith rather than microservices or an unstructured single project.

This demonstrates that architecture decisions should preserve reasoning, alternatives and consequences.

## Key Takeaways

- Decision records should capture significant choices.
- Not every choice needs a formal record.
- Architecture decisions should explain trade-offs.
