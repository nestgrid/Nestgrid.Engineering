# Philosophy

> Part of the **[Lifecycle Mini Sample](../../README.md)**.

## Purpose

The sample product exists to help small teams maintain task visibility without introducing heavy project management process.

The engineering philosophy is to keep the product simple, understandable and easy to operate.

## Guidance

The product should solve a narrow problem clearly.

It should prioritise clarity, maintainability and low operational overhead over advanced workflow features.

### Engineering Principles Applied

- Understand the team coordination problem before designing features.
- Prefer simple workflows over configurable complexity.
- Keep important decisions visible through decision records.
- Design for maintainability before scale becomes a concern.

### Trade-off Posture

The sample accepts limited flexibility in exchange for lower complexity.

Advanced automation, custom workflows and deep integrations are intentionally deferred.

## Key Takeaways

- Philosophy sets the decision posture for the sample.
- Simplicity is an explicit product and engineering choice.
- Deferred complexity should be deliberate, not accidental.
