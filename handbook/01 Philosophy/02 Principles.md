# Principles

> Part of the **[Philosophy](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Engineering principles provide a consistent foundation for making decisions throughout the software lifecycle.

Unlike technologies, frameworks or methodologies, principles remain stable over time. They guide engineers towards solutions that are maintainable, understandable and aligned with business objectives.

## Guidance

Every engineering decision should be evaluated against a common set of principles. While individual projects may differ in complexity or technology, the principles that guide engineering remain consistent.

### Solve the Right Problem

Understand the business problem before designing the solution.

Avoid optimising or implementing solutions for problems that have not been clearly understood.

### Simplicity Over Complexity

Prefer the simplest solution that satisfies the requirements.

Complexity should only be introduced where it delivers clear and measurable value.

### Design with Intent

Every component, interface and dependency should exist for a clear reason.

Avoid accidental architecture and unnecessary abstraction.

### Consistency Builds Confidence

Consistent approaches reduce cognitive load, improve collaboration and make systems easier to maintain.

Where standards exist, they should be followed unless there is a justified reason to deviate.

### Quality by Design

Quality should be considered throughout the engineering lifecycle rather than inspected at the end.

Reliability, security, maintainability and testability should influence every engineering decision.

### Continuous Learning

Engineering is an evolving discipline.

Engineers should continuously improve their knowledge, challenge assumptions and refine practices based on experience and evidence.

## Key Takeaways

- Understand the problem before designing the solution.
- Prefer simplicity wherever practical.
- Design intentionally rather than accidentally.
- Consistency improves maintainability.
- Build quality into the solution from the beginning.
- Commit to continuous improvement.
