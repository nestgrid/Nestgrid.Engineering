# Sample Quality

> Part of the **[Samples](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Sample quality defines what makes a sample trustworthy as a reference.

Samples should demonstrate the standards expected elsewhere in the methodology.

## Guidance

A sample that teaches poor structure, unclear naming or weak documentation can cause more harm than no sample.

Samples should be reviewed as carefully as guidance documents.

### Follow the Methodology

Samples should reflect the engineering lifecycle and standards they demonstrate.

They should not contradict the guidance without explaining why.

### Be Clear and Navigable

Samples should be easy to inspect.

Readers should understand where to start, what to read next and what the sample is demonstrating.

### Prefer Correctness Over Completeness

A sample does not need to include every possible feature.

It should be correct and coherent within its stated scope.

### Include Relevant Tests or Validation

Where a sample includes implementation, it should show appropriate testing or validation.

This reinforces quality expectations.

### State Limitations

Samples should identify important limitations where they exist.

This prevents readers from assuming the sample is production-ready in areas it was not designed to cover.

## Key Takeaways

- Samples should be trustworthy references.
- They should follow the methodology they demonstrate.
- Navigation and clarity matter.
- Correctness is more important than breadth.
- Implementation samples should include relevant validation.
- Limitations should be visible.
