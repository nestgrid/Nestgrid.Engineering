# Decision Quality

> Part of the **[Decisions](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Decision quality defines what makes a decision record useful, trustworthy and maintainable.

It helps teams avoid vague, incomplete or decorative records that do not support future understanding.

## Guidance

A good decision record should help a future reader understand the choice without needing to reconstruct old conversations.

It should be concise, honest and specific.

### Be Specific

The decision should clearly state what was decided.

Avoid broad statements that sound useful but do not guide action.

### Include Enough Context

Context should explain why the decision was needed.

Too little context makes the decision hard to evaluate later.

### Explain Alternatives

Important alternatives should be mentioned, especially when they were plausible.

This helps readers understand why the chosen option was preferred.

### State Consequences Honestly

Decision records should include accepted costs and risks.

Hiding downsides weakens trust and makes future maintenance harder.

### Keep It Brief

Decision records should not become essays.

If the topic requires deep supporting material, link to it rather than placing everything in the record.

## Key Takeaways

- A useful decision record is specific and honest.
- Context explains why the decision was needed.
- Alternatives clarify trade-offs.
- Consequences should include costs and risks.
- Brevity keeps records readable.
