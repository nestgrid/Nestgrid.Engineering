# Sample Purpose

> Part of the **[Samples](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Sample purpose defines why samples exist and what they should help readers learn.

Samples provide practical examples of the methodology applied to realistic engineering artefacts.

## Guidance

Samples should make abstract guidance concrete.

They should help readers understand how to apply the lifecycle, not merely show isolated files.

### Demonstrate Practice

Samples should show how methodology becomes real artefacts.

They may demonstrate documentation, domain modelling, decisions, structure, tests or implementation patterns.

### Teach Clearly

Each sample should have a clear learning purpose.

Readers should be able to understand what the sample is intended to demonstrate.

### Stay Product-Neutral Where Appropriate

Samples should not depend on private or product-specific knowledge unless the sample is intentionally tied to a specific product.

General samples should remain reusable across contexts.

### Show Complete Enough Context

Samples should include enough surrounding context to make the example understandable.

Overly isolated snippets can be misleading.

### Avoid Copy-Paste Thinking

Samples are references, not universal answers.

Readers should adapt the lesson to their own context.

## Key Takeaways

- Samples turn guidance into practical examples.
- Each sample should teach something specific.
- General samples should remain product-neutral.
- Samples need enough context to be useful.
- Samples should guide adaptation, not blind copying.
