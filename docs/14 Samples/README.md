# Samples

The Samples stage defines how example artefacts and reference implementations should demonstrate the engineering methodology in practice.

Samples help readers move from guidance to application. They show what good structure, documentation, modelling, decisions and implementation can look like.

Samples should be realistic enough to teach, but focused enough to remain understandable.

## Purpose

To establish guidance for creating and maintaining samples that demonstrate the Nestgrid Engineering Lifecycle clearly and consistently.

## Objectives

Upon completing this book, engineers should be able to:

- Understand the role of samples in the methodology.
- Design samples that teach specific concepts clearly.
- Keep samples realistic without unnecessary complexity.
- Maintain samples as standards evolve.
- Use samples as references without copying blindly.
