# Architecture Handover

This initiative captures the refinement that separates Discovery approval from Discovery-to-Architecture handover.

## Context

While applying Nestgrid Engineering to new products, the Product Brief proved useful as the Discovery approval artefact.

However, Architecture also benefits from a short final handover that explains what Architecture should do next without requiring the architect to infer the transition from the full Product Brief.

## Scope

This initiative updates the Engineering Handbook and artefact templates to introduce Architecture Handover as a standard Discovery output.

## Changes

- Added Architecture Handover as a standard artefact template.
- Clarified that the Product Brief is the Discovery approval artefact.
- Clarified that Architecture Handover is the final Discovery handover artefact.
- Updated handover commands, review gates, role outputs, repository storage guidance and samples.
