# Opportunity Exploration

This initiative captures the refinement that makes early product discovery explicitly conversational and decision-oriented.

## Context

The Product Owner role and Evelyn profile were updated to make Discovery collaborative and evidence-led.

A related personal EOS implementation introduced a fuller Opportunity Exploration workflow, an optional Opportunity Decision artefact and a split Discovery Gate.

Those ideas fit the Nestgrid Engineering Operating System and reduce the risk that Product Discovery becomes document generation.

## Objective

Incorporate Opportunity Exploration into the Nestgrid Engineering Operating System without changing the lifecycle stages or weakening the Product Brief as the approved Product Definition artefact.

## Scope

- Add an Opportunity Exploration workflow.
- Add an optional Opportunity Decision artefact template.
- Split Discovery Gate guidance into Opportunity Decision and Product Definition checkpoints.
- Update Discovery methodology guidance.
- Update Product Owner, Evelyn and prompt guidance where needed.
- Record the architecture decision.
