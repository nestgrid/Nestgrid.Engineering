## Purpose

Summarise the incorporation of Opportunity Exploration into the Nestgrid Engineering Operating System.

## Scope

- Added an Opportunity Exploration workflow.
- Added an optional Opportunity Decision artefact template.
- Updated Discovery methodology and review gate guidance.
- Updated Product Owner and Evelyn guidance to recognise build, reuse, defer and no-build outcomes.
- Updated prompt samples so Evelyn can lead early idea exploration before Product Brief production.
- Added an Architecture Decision Record.

## Implementation Summary

Opportunity Exploration is now recognised as the standard entry point for early ideas and observed problems.

The Discovery Gate now distinguishes between Opportunity Decision and Product Definition.

The Product Brief remains the approved Product Definition artefact once the opportunity is worth formal definition.

## Validation

Local Markdown links were validated after the update.

## Outcome

The Product Owner can now complete valuable Discovery work without forcing every idea into a product artefact.

Evelyn can act as a collaborative, tool-enabled thinking partner before deciding whether a Product Brief is justified.

## Recommendations

Use Opportunity Exploration for vague, early or speculative ideas.

Use New Product when a positive Opportunity Decision or explicit Project Sponsor direction already exists.
