# Incident Response

> Part of the **[Operations](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Incident response defines how teams respond when production behaviour threatens users, business outcomes or system health.

It helps teams restore service, communicate clearly and learn from failure.

## Guidance

Incidents should be handled calmly, visibly and systematically.

The immediate goal is to reduce impact. The later goal is to understand and improve.

### Define Incident Severity

Severity levels should describe impact and urgency.

Clear severity helps teams prioritise response and communication.

### Assign Roles

Incident response should identify who leads, investigates, communicates and coordinates.

Roles reduce confusion during stressful situations.

### Communicate Clearly

Stakeholders should receive timely, accurate and appropriate updates.

Communication should explain impact, current status and expected next steps without speculation.

### Restore Service First

During an active incident, restoring acceptable service usually matters more than finding perfect root cause.

Root cause analysis can continue after impact is reduced.

### Review Without Blame

Post-incident review should focus on learning and system improvement.

The goal is to understand contributing factors and reduce future risk.

## Key Takeaways

- Incident response should be calm and systematic.
- Severity helps prioritise response.
- Roles reduce confusion.
- Communication should be timely and factual.
- Restoring service is the immediate priority.
- Reviews should focus on learning, not blame.
