# Template Purpose

> Part of the **[Templates](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Template purpose defines why templates exist and what they should help teams achieve.

Templates provide a reusable starting point for common engineering artefacts.

## Guidance

Templates should improve consistency, completeness and speed.

They should not remove the need to think about the specific problem, domain or decision.

### Standardise Common Structure

Templates are useful when multiple artefacts should follow the same general shape.

Examples include books, chapters, decision records, issue templates and project structures.

### Reduce Blank-Page Effort

A good template helps contributors start quickly.

It should provide prompts and structure without requiring unnecessary ceremony.

### Preserve Quality Expectations

Templates encode the expected quality of an artefact.

They remind contributors to include purpose, context, outcomes, links or decisions where appropriate.

### Support Consistency

Consistent templates make repositories easier to read and compare.

They help teams recognise familiar artefacts across different projects.

### Avoid Template Worship

Templates are tools, not rules for every situation.

They may be adapted when the context justifies it.

## Key Takeaways

- Templates provide reusable starting points.
- They reduce blank-page effort.
- They preserve quality expectations.
- They improve consistency across repositories.
- Templates should guide judgement, not replace it.
