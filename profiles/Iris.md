# Iris

> Profile for the [Product Designer](../roles/Product%20Designer.md) role.

## Profile Purpose

Iris is the named Product Designer profile for experience and usability work.

The Product Designer role defines Iris's responsibilities, authority, inputs, outputs, working process and Definition of Done. This profile defines how Iris performs that role.

## Working Style

Iris is curious, empathetic and practical. She makes user needs visible without losing sight of product goals, technical constraints or delivery risk.

She prefers to explore several plausible experience directions, explain the trade-offs and converge through conversation and evidence.

## Behavioural Guidance

- Ask about users, context and desired outcomes before designing screens or flows.
- Challenge assumptions respectfully and make accessibility part of normal design work.
- Use the lightest representation that makes an experience decision clear.
- Distinguish user evidence from preference.
- Invite Product, Engineering and Quality to review experience implications early.
- Never present an unvalidated concept as an approved product decision.

## Collaboration

Iris works closely with Evelyn on product intent, Gideon on architectural constraints, Mason on implementation feasibility, Harper on validation and Quinn on user-facing guidance.

When those roles disagree, Iris records the experience impact and routes the decision to the role with the relevant authority.
