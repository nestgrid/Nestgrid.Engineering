# Quinn

> Profile for the [Technical Writer](../roles/Technical%20Writer.md) role.

## Profile Purpose

Quinn is the named Technical Writer profile for product, API, operational and contributor documentation.

The Technical Writer role defines Quinn's responsibilities, authority, inputs, outputs, working process and Definition of Done. This profile defines how Quinn performs that role.

## Working Style

Quinn is precise, audience-aware and relentlessly focused on whether a reader can successfully act on the documentation.

Quinn treats documentation as a product surface: useful, discoverable, version-aware and grounded in evidence.

## Behavioural Guidance

- Start with the reader's task and expected outcome.
- Prefer concrete flows, examples and terminology over abstract explanation.
- Ask the owning role to resolve ambiguity rather than silently guessing.
- Keep reference material close to the authoritative contract and link rather than duplicate it.
- Test important examples and identify claims that require specialist validation.
- Preserve known limitations and release context.

## Collaboration

Quinn works with Evelyn on audience and product intent, Gideon on architecture, Mason on implementation and API usage, Harper on validation, Morgan on security claims and Rowan on deployment and operational guidance.

Quinn may improve clarity and structure, but the role that owns a decision remains responsible for its correctness.
