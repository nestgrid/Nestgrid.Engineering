# Engineering Role Profiles

Engineering role profiles are part of the Nestgrid Engineering Operating System.

They define the responsibilities, behaviours and decision-making principles that govern every stage of the Nestgrid Engineering Lifecycle.

They are not only AI prompts, job descriptions or implementation guides.

They are engineering disciplines.

The Engineering Handbook and workflows define roles. People, agents or automation may perform those roles.

Each role owns a distinct stage or responsibility in the lifecycle and is accountable for reducing uncertainty before handing responsibility to the next role.

Together the role profiles provide a consistent operating model that is independent of technology, implementation tools and AI platforms.

# Shared Philosophy

Although each role owns a different discipline, they all share the same engineering philosophy.

Every role should:

- understand before acting;
- ask questions before making assumptions;
- assess before recommending;
- agree an approach before executing;
- execute intentionally;
- review their own work objectively;
- complete their responsibilities fully;
- and prepare the next role for success.

Engineering is a collaborative lifecycle rather than a sequence of isolated activities.

---

# Common Working Process

Every role follows the same working process unless its profile explicitly narrows the scope.

1. Review
2. Understand
3. Question
4. Assess
5. Recommend
6. Execute
7. Review
8. Complete
9. Handover

This consistency allows both people and agents to work predictably throughout the engineering lifecycle whilst allowing each role to specialise within its own discipline.

---

# Stewardship

Every role is responsible not only for completing its own stage, but also for preparing the next stage of the lifecycle.

The quality of a role is measured not only by the work it produces, but by the confidence it provides to every role that follows.

Successful engineering is collaborative rather than sequential.

---

# Ownership

Roles own lifecycle stages rather than individual activities.

They remain accountable regardless of whether work is performed by:

- engineers;
- engineering teams;
- AI systems;
- automation tools;
- or any combination thereof.

Implementation may evolve.

Ownership remains constant.

---

# Engineering Artefacts

Documentation, source code, tests, infrastructure, reports and other deliverables are not the objective of the Engineering Lifecycle.

They are evidence that a lifecycle stage has been completed successfully.

Each role determines the artefacts appropriate for its discipline whilst remaining aligned with the Engineering Handbook.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Roles continuously improve both the solution and the engineering methodology.

Where opportunities for improvement are identified, roles should raise feedback rather than silently compensating for shortcomings elsewhere in the lifecycle.

The Engineering Handbook evolves through the collective learning of every role.

---

# Engineering Excellence

The role profiles exist to help teams deliver software that is:

- intentional;
- understandable;
- maintainable;
- secure;
- reliable;
- observable;
- and capable of evolving with confidence.

The objective is not simply to produce software.

The objective is to build enduring engineering systems that people and AI can develop together with professionalism, consistency and trust.

# Our Engineering Organisation
The role profiles define the engineering culture of Nestgrid.
They establish the behaviours, responsibilities and decision-making principles expected of every engineer, whether human or AI.
New role profiles may be introduced as the organisation evolves, but they will always share the same commitment to intentional engineering, professional stewardship and continuous improvement.
Technology changes.
Tools evolve.
Engineering endures.
