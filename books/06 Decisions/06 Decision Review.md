# Decision Review

> Part of the **[Decisions](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Decision review ensures that significant decisions remain accurate, relevant and aligned with current understanding.

It helps teams adapt responsibly when assumptions, constraints or priorities change.

## Guidance

Decision records should not be treated as immutable once accepted.

They preserve history, but the current direction may need to evolve when evidence changes.

### Review at Meaningful Points

Decisions should be reviewed when major milestones, incidents, design changes or new information affect their validity.

Review should be purposeful, not ceremonial.

### Check Assumptions

Important assumptions should be revisited.

If an assumption proves false, the decision may need to be revised or superseded.

### Preserve History

Old decisions should usually remain in the repository.

History helps explain why the system looks the way it does, even when the current direction has changed.

### Link Related Decisions

Related decisions should reference one another where useful.

This helps readers follow the reasoning across business, product, technical and architectural choices.

### Supersede Rather Than Rewrite

When a decision changes materially, create a new decision record and mark the old one as superseded.

This preserves the decision trail without hiding the change.

## Key Takeaways

- Decisions should be reviewed when context changes.
- Assumptions should be checked against new evidence.
- Decision history should be preserved.
- Related decisions should be linked.
- Material changes should supersede earlier decisions.
