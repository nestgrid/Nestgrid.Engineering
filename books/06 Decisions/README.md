# Decisions

The Decisions stage records important choices made throughout the engineering lifecycle.

Decision records preserve context, rationale, alternatives and consequences so future engineers can understand why something was chosen, not only what was chosen.

Decisions are not limited to architecture. Business, product, technical and architectural decisions may all affect the direction and maintainability of a solution.

## Purpose

To capture significant decisions in a clear, traceable and durable form so that teams can preserve reasoning and revisit choices responsibly.

## Objectives

Upon completing this book, engineers should be able to:

- Identify decisions that should be recorded.
- Distinguish between business, product, technical and architectural decisions.
- Write decision records with useful context and rationale.
- Track decision status and consequences.
- Review and supersede decisions as understanding changes.
