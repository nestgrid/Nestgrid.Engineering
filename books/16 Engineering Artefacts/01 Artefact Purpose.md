# Artefact Purpose

> Part of the **[Engineering Artefacts](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Artefacts provide durable context for engineering decisions and handovers.

## Guidance

An artefact should explain the current state of understanding clearly enough that another role can continue the work.

Artefacts are not bureaucracy. They are the working memory of the engineering lifecycle.

### Preserve Context

Artefacts capture what matters from discovery, design, implementation, testing, security and operations.

They prevent important decisions from being trapped in chat history or meeting notes.

### Enable Review

Artefacts make work reviewable.

Reviewers can assess scope, assumptions, risks and decisions without reconstructing the full discussion.

### Support Traceability

Artefacts should link to related decisions, pull requests, product backlog items, releases and previous versions.

This helps teams understand how a solution evolved.

## Key Takeaways

- Artefacts preserve context.
- Artefacts make work reviewable.
- Artefacts support handover between roles.
- Artefacts should be useful, concise and traceable.
