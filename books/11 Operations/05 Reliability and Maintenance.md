# Reliability and Maintenance

> Part of the **[Operations](README.md)**.

## Purpose

Reliability and maintenance define how systems remain dependable and healthy over time.

They ensure production quality is preserved after initial delivery.

## Guidance

Reliability is not achieved once and then forgotten.

It requires ongoing attention to failures, dependencies, capacity, data, security, performance and technical health.

### Define Reliability Expectations

Teams should understand what level of reliability the system requires.

Expectations should align with business impact and user needs.

### Maintain Dependencies

Dependencies should be reviewed and updated responsibly.

Unsupported or vulnerable dependencies increase operational risk.

### Manage Technical Health

Maintenance should include refactoring, cleanup, dependency updates, documentation updates and removal of obsolete behaviour.

Ignoring technical health makes future delivery slower and riskier.

### Monitor Capacity

Systems should be observed for capacity and performance trends.

Growth, load changes and resource constraints should be identified before they become incidents.

### Plan Regular Review

Operational health should be reviewed periodically.

Reviews should consider incidents, alerts, support issues, reliability trends and maintenance backlog.

## Key Takeaways

- Reliability requires ongoing attention.
- Reliability expectations should match business impact.
- Dependencies must be maintained.
- Technical health is operational work.
- Capacity trends should be monitored.
- Regular review helps prevent decay.
