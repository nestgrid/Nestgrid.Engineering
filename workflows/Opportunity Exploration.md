# Opportunity Exploration

## Purpose

Explore an early idea collaboratively and decide whether it deserves product, library or existing-product work.

This workflow is conversational and decision-oriented. It does not assume that a product exists and does not require a Product Brief.

## Participants

1. Product Owner
2. Project Sponsor or idea owner
3. Other roles only when a focused uncertainty requires their input

## Flow

```text
Idea or observed problem
  -> Open conversation
  -> Questions and hypotheses
  -> Evidence and relevant tool use
  -> Alternative framings
  -> Shared understanding
  -> Opportunity Decision
      -> New Product or Library
      -> Existing Product Change
      -> Reframe or Research
      -> Use Existing Solution
      -> Defer
      -> Stop
```

Conversation may move backward as evidence changes the framing. Do not manufacture linear progress when the idea remains uncertain.

## Tool Use

The Product Owner may inspect repositories and artefacts, research current alternatives, use authorised connected context, analyse data or trade-offs, and recommend lightweight experiments.

Use only tools that materially reduce an important uncertainty.

Separate sourced evidence from inference.

Do not perform state-changing external actions without authority.

## Expected Outputs

- Shared understanding.
- Verbal or durable Opportunity Decision.
- Focused questions, research or experiment recommendations where needed.

Use the [Opportunity Decision template](../templates/artefacts/Opportunity%20Decision.Template.md) only when the rationale should be preserved.

## Approval Gate

Apply the Opportunity Decision checkpoint in [Review Gates](../books/15%20Engineering%20Workflow/05%20Review%20Gates.md#checkpoint-1-opportunity-decision).

If the outcome is a new product or library, transition to [New Product](New%20Product.md).

If the outcome is a change to an existing product, transition to the appropriate existing-product workflow.

Other outcomes end, pause or redirect the initiative without requiring downstream lifecycle artefacts.

## Principles

- Conversation before documentation.
- Explore before narrowing.
- Use evidence without outsourcing judgement to tools.
- Do not create a project merely to justify the discussion.
- Stopping can be the highest-value outcome.
