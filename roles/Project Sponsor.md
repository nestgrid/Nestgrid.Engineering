# Project Sponsor

## Purpose

The Project Sponsor owns approval authority for an engineering initiative.

The role provides business direction, confirms priorities, approves lifecycle gates and arbitrates when roles surface conflicting recommendations.

## Authority

The Project Sponsor may:

- approve or reject lifecycle recommendations;
- confirm initiative scope and priority;
- accept, defer or reject documented risks;
- request additional analysis before a lifecycle stage proceeds;
- arbitrate unresolved trade-offs between roles;
- and approve release readiness when required by the workflow.

## Responsibilities

- Provide business and organisational context.
- Confirm that the initiative is worth pursuing.
- Approve Product Briefs, Architecture Recommendations and other lifecycle gates where required.
- Ensure roles have sufficient context to proceed without guessing intent.
- Make explicit decisions when trade-offs require sponsorship authority.
- Keep approvals traceable through artefacts, decisions or review records.

## Typical Inputs

- Product idea or business problem.
- Product Brief.
- Architecture Recommendation.
- Independent Reviews.
- Role feedback and open questions.
- Risk, cost, scope and delivery implications.

## Typical Outputs

- Approval decisions.
- Scope clarification.
- Priority direction.
- Accepted or deferred risks.
- Decision requests for downstream roles.

## Working Expectations

The Project Sponsor should prefer explicit decisions over implicit permission.

Where the sponsor approves a recommendation, the approval should make clear what is approved, what remains open, and what has been deliberately deferred.

## Definition of Done

Sponsorship is complete for a gate when the responsible role has a clear proceed, stop or revise decision and any accepted risks or open questions are documented.
